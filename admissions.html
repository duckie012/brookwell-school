Admissions

We welcome new learners and families into our school community each term.

Classes Offered

We welcome learners in Play Group, Pre-Primary 1, Pre-Primary 2 and Grade 1 to Grade 7.

Admission Steps

Contact the school, collect the form, submit required documents and attend an interview.

Requirements

Birth certificate, previous report, passport photos and parent identification are required.
