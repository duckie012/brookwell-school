About Brookwell Harmony School

We are a vibrant school community committed to helping every child learn with confidence and joy from Play Group to Grade 7.

About the School

Brookwell Harmony School is a day primary school based in Nanyuki. We welcome learners from Nanyuki town and the surrounding areas, offering a safe and nurturing campus for young children to grow.

We also provide school bus transport for students, making it easier for families across town and nearby neighborhoods to join our community.

Our school follows the Competency-Based Education (CBE) curriculum and offers all standard classes, including Mathematics, English, Kiswahili, Science, Dancing, CRE and Social Studies.

With a strong focus on both academic success and extracurricular balance, Brookwell Harmony School helps each learner develop the knowledge, skills and confidence needed for primary school success.
